INTRODUCTION
0702805881 chirathdinuka20030627@gmail.com
Building a house in Sri Lanka is a complex and stressful process.
In Sri Lanka, building a house is one of the biggest dreams in a person’s life,
but at the same time, it is one of the most difficult and stressful processes
that people face. Many people already have land, but they do not know how
to start building their dream home. They are confused about where to go first,
whether they should meet an architect, engineer, contractor, or a lawyer. Even
if they start the process, they do not have a clear idea about the total cost,
the quality of materials, the labor charges, or the time required to complete
the construction. Because of this lack of knowledge and guidance, many
people end up spending more money than expected, choosing the wrong
workers, or facing delays and legal issues. There is also no single platform in
Sri Lanka that connects all these services together in one place. People have
to go to many different places to get information, compare prices, and find
professionals. This creates a big gap in the construction industry. Therefore,
there is a strong need for a smart, simple, and centralized system that can
guide people from the beginning of their construction journey until the final
completion of their home. BuildMe.lk is designed to solve this problem by
creating a complete digital solution where users can plan, manage, and build
their dream home easily using their mobile phone or computer.
Most people have land but they don’t know:
Not knowing the total cost
Not understanding materials and quality
Not finding trusted workers
Not knowing legal procedures
Difficulty in managing time and construction process
Lack of transparency in pricing
PROJECT IDEA
0702805881 chirathdinuka20030627@gmail.com
BuildMe.lk is a smart digital platform (website + mobile app) that connects
everything needed to build a house into one system
The idea of BuildMe.lk starts from a very simple real-life situation: a person
has land and a dream to build a house, but they do not know how to begin.
Instead of going to different people and wasting time and money, they open
the BuildMe.lk website or mobile app. They log in easily using their Telegram or
email account, and then they upload a simple sketch of their dream house or
enter basic details such as the number of rooms, floors, and size. The system
then automatically generates a rough estimation, including total cost,
required materials like cement, sand, steel, and wires, and even the estimated
time to complete the project. After that, the user can publish their project,
and instantly, all registered professionals such as contractors, architects,
plumbers, electricians, and engineers receive a notification that a new project
is available. These professionals can then start bidding on the project by
offering their price and services, just like a tender system. One contractor may
offer 10 million rupees, another may offer 9.5 million, and another may offer
9.2 million, creating a competitive environment where the customer can
choose the best option based on price, quality, and ratings. At the same time,
the platform also provides additional services such as legal advice, worker
hiring, material purchasing, and transportation booking. Even if a construction
site suddenly needs workers in the morning, they can post a job, and available
workers can accept it instantly. This system acts like a “Google for
construction in Sri Lanka,” where everything related to building a house is
available in one place. The goal is to make the entire construction process
simple, fast, transparent, and affordable for everyone.

The bidding system is the core feature and biggest innovation of
BuildMe.lk. This system works like an open tender process where
multiple professionals compete for a project. After a user publishes
their project, all registered contractors and service providers receive
a notification. They can then submit their bids by offering a price and
explaining their services. This creates a competitive environment
where professionals try to offer better prices and better quality to
win the project. The user can view all bids in a clear format and
compare them based on price, ratings, experience, and past work.
Instead of choosing only the lowest price, the user can select the
best overall option. This system helps reduce construction costs and
improves service quality because professionals must compete to win
projects. It also increases transparency and trust between customers
and service providers.
0702805881 chirathdinuka20030627@gmail.com
To prevent users from bypassing the platform and contacting
professionals directly, BuildMe.lk uses a ticket-based system. Before
accessing full details or contacting contractors, users must purchase
a ticket. This ticket acts as a secure access key that allows users to
view bids, communicate with professionals, and proceed with the
project. This system ensures that all transactions happen within the
platform, which helps maintain fairness and also generates revenue
for the company.
